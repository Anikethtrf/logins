Install the extension

  1. Download and unzip the project package.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, edge://extensions, or brave://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode, then choose Load unpacked.
  4. Select the extension folder. Pin Pocket Vault to the toolbar.
  5. Open it, sign in, unlock your vault, and enable login detection.

This extension is for desktop Chromium browsers. The website works on phones, but mobile Chrome does not load these extensions.

UNDER LOCK AND KEY

Your vault's security

What is stored, and what stays with you.

Encrypted on your device

Your login titles, websites, usernames, passwords, and notes are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Your master password derives the encryption key with PBKDF2-SHA-256 and 600,000 iterations.

The cloud stores encrypted records, a random salt, timestamps, and your account ID. Your account email is managed by the sign-in service.

A separate master password

It is never sent to the sign-in service. Account password recovery does not recover this encryption key.

Automatic locking

The vault locks after 5 minutes without interaction. The website clears its key when you reload or close it. Sessions require a new sign-in after eight hours. Extension unlock state lives in browser-session memory and is cleared on browser restart.

Before using real passwords

This is a custom prototype, not an independently audited password manager. Test it with throwaway logins first. A compromised device or modified website code can read passwords while your vault is unlocked.

The backup includes encrypted records and key settings, never your master password. Restore currently requires developer tools.
